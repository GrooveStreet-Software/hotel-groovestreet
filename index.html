About Hotel GrooveStreet

A premier destination in the heart of Uganda

Hotel GrooveStreet is a premier luxury destination in the heart of Uganda that combines contemporary elegance with warm African hospitality. Located near the vibrant capital of Kampala, we offer easy access to national parks, cultural sites, and business districts while providing a tranquil retreat from the bustling urban environment.

  • Premium location in Uganda's capital region
  • Award-winning dining with local Ugandan flavors
  • Gateway to gorilla trekking and wildlife safaris
  • Exceptional customer service 24/7
